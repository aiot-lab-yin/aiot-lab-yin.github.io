On **October 12, 2026**, **Dr. Yin Chen** led the AIoT Laboratory team from Reitaku University to Shanghai, China, for **ACM UbiComp/ISWC 2026**, a leading international conference in ubiquitous computing. The team delivered a **3.5-hour, in-person tutorial** on reproducible Wi-Fi radio frequency (RF) fingerprinting. The tutorial proposal received a **top review score of 5/5** during the conference's peer-review process.

The tutorial, **“Building Reproducible Wi-Fi RF Fingerprinting Pipelines: Signal Collection, Datasets, and Evaluation,”** introduced an experimental workflow covering Wi-Fi signal acquisition, RF feature extraction, device identification, and performance evaluation. Built on the team's publicly released **SMoRFFI dataset and open-source framework**, the session combined a walkthrough of the USRP/GNU Radio acquisition system with hands-on notebook exercises using prepared Wi-Fi records. Participants explored RF features, visualized device characteristics, and reproduced a baseline identification experiment.

**Jinxiao Zhu** served as the lead instructor. **Yin Chen, Zhen Jia, Kosuke Watanabe, and Wenhao Huang** provided on-site teaching assistance and technical support throughout the session.

During the coffee break and hands-on activities, the team exchanged ideas with participants from different countries and research backgrounds. Discussions focused on practical challenges in RF fingerprinting, experiment reproducibility, and potential research directions.
